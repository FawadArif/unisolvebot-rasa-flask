About UniSolveBot

UniSolveBot is a chatbot developed to assist university students with their queries related to admission, registration, re-enrollment, examinations, and more. Our aim is to provide quick and accurate responses to help students navigate university policies with ease.

Features of UniSolveBot include:

  • 24/7 Assistance: Get help anytime you need it, day or night.
  • Quick and Accurate Responses: Receive precise answers to your questions in seconds.
  • Easy to Use: User-friendly interface that makes navigation a breeze.
  • Supports Multiple Queries: Handles a variety of questions across different topics.

Our goal is to make your university experience as smooth and stress-free as possible.
